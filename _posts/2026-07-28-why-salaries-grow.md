<p>
A great contract should matter.
</p>

<p>
It just shouldn't stay great forever.
</p>

<p>
IceCore allows General Managers to find value through the draft, waivers,
prospect development and long-term roster building.
</p>

<p>
Find a player before everybody else and you should benefit.
</p>

<p>
But if that player becomes one of the best assets in the league,
his salary eventually has to follow.
</p>

<p>
That is why keeper salaries grow.
</p>

<h2>Two Paths</h2>

<p>
When a regular player is protected for another season, his salary follows
a simple two-part system.
</p>

<div class="ic-article-stat-grid">
  <div class="ic-article-stat">
    <strong>+4</strong>
    <span>Every regular keeper</span>
  </div>

  <div class="ic-article-stat">
    <strong>+ Value</strong>
    <span>Performance-based bonus</span>
  </div>
</div>

<p>
Every regular keeper receives a 4-Coin increase.
</p>

<p>
Some regular keepers may also receive a 5-Coin <strong>IceCore Value Bonus</strong>.
The bonus is available to underpaid players based on their final Yahoo Fantasy
Points ranking and the actual salary economy of their performance group.
</p>

<p>
The Top 150 skaters are divided into groups of 10. The Top 10 goaltenders form
one separate performance group. For each group, the highest and lowest salary
are removed, and the average of the remaining salaries becomes that group's
benchmark.
</p>

<p>
A player receives a Value Bonus when his previous salary is below the benchmark
of his performance group. The bonus is calculated from the salary gap rather
than being a fixed amount.
</p>

<p>
There are no fixed salary thresholds and no commissioner-assigned player values.
The benchmark is created by the actual salaries paid by IceCore franchises,
so the league's own market determines what represents an underpaid contract.
</p>

<p>
The maximum regular keeper increase is therefore <strong>9 Coins</strong>:
the Base Increase plus the calculated Value Bonus.
</p>

<p>
The <strong>IceCore Value Board</strong> will be published on the league website
at <a href="{{ '/value-board/' | relative_url }}">Value Board</a>. During the
season, the page may contain informative interim lists of players who would
currently appear likely to qualify for a Value Bonus based on the information
available at that time. These lists are informational only. Final Value Bonus
recipients are determined after the completed regular season using the final
Yahoo Fantasy point rankings and the final applicable salaries.
</p>

<h2>The Normal Keeper</h2>

<p>
Start with a useful established player carrying a 34-Coin salary.
</p>

<p>
He remains productive, but is not selected for the Value Bonus.
</p>

<div class="ic-article-flow">
  <span>34</span>
  <span>→</span>
  <strong>38</strong>
  <span>→</span>
  <strong>42</strong>
  <span>→</span>
  <strong>46</strong>
</div>

<p>
Nothing dramatic happens.
</p>

<p>
Every offseason the General Manager pays another 4 Coins to keep him.
</p>

<p>
Eventually the question changes.
</p>

<p>
Not:
</p>

<blockquote>
Is he a good player?
</blockquote>

<p>
But:
</p>

<blockquote>
Is he still worth keeping at this salary?
</blockquote>

<p>
That decision is the point.
</p>

<h2>The Breakout</h2>

<p>
Now imagine a player already carrying a salary of just 1 Coin.
</p>

<p>
He breaks out and finishes inside the Top 150. More importantly, his salary
is below the average salary of his performance group after the highest and
lowest salaries are removed.
</p>

<div class="ic-article-flow">
  <span>1</span>
  <span>→</span>
  <strong>10</strong>
</div>

<p>
The regular keeper receives the current Base Increase, normally 4 Coins.
The Value Bonus is then calculated from the gap between his salary and the
benchmark of his final performance group.
</p>

<p>
The player was discovered early, and the franchise benefits from that value.
But the increase is determined by the player's actual production relative
to his salary — not by a commissioner-set salary threshold.
</p>

<p>
If his salary remains below the benchmark of his new performance group in
another completed season, he may again qualify for a Value Bonus. The amount
depends on the new gap, not on a fixed bonus.
</p>

<p>
The market decides.
</p>

<h2>The Waiver Discovery</h2>

<p>
Some of the best fantasy stories begin with a player nobody owned.
</p>

<p>
A player entering the IceCore economy without an established salary
can be acquired from waivers at 0 Coins.
</p>

<p>
If he remains outside the selected Value Bonus group and you protect him:
</p>

<div class="ic-article-flow">
  <span>0</span>
  <span>→</span>
  <strong>4</strong>
</div>

<p>
If his completed season makes him eligible for the IceCore Value Bonus:
</p>

<div class="ic-article-flow">
  <span>0</span>
  <span>→</span>
  <strong>9</strong>
</div>

<p>
You found the player for nothing.
</p>

<p>
You still get the bargain — but exceptional value is eventually reflected
in the contract.
</p>

<h2>The Dropped Contract</h2>

<p>
Releasing a player does not erase his IceCore salary.
</p>

<p>
Imagine a player carrying a 10-Coin salary.
</p>

<p>
His franchise drops him and another General Manager claims him.
</p>

<div class="ic-article-flow">
  <span>10</span>
  <span>→</span>
  <span>DROPPED</span>
  <span>→</span>
  <strong>10</strong>
</div>

<p>
He does not become a new 0-Coin asset.
</p>

<p>
His established salary follows his IceCore history.
</p>

<p>
If the new General Manager protects him after an ordinary season:
</p>

<div class="ic-article-flow">
  <span>10</span>
  <span>→</span>
  <strong>14</strong>
</div>

<p>
If his completed season also makes him eligible for a Value Bonus, the total
increase is calculated from the benchmark gap rather than a fixed +9:
</p>

<div class="ic-article-flow">
  <span>10</span>
  <span>→</span>
  <strong>19</strong>
</div>

<p>
Changing franchises changes the owner.
</p>

<p>
It does not reset the contract.
</p>

<h2>The Expensive Star</h2>

<p>
Salary growth is not only about cheap players.
</p>

<p>
Imagine an established superstar already carrying a 94-Coin salary.
</p>

<div class="ic-article-flow">
  <span>94</span>
  <span>→</span>
  <strong>98</strong>
  <span>→</span>
  <strong>102</strong>
  <span>→</span>
  <strong>106</strong>
</div>

<p>
The Base Increase is normally 4 Coins and does not automatically become larger
just because the player has a high salary. The Value Bonus depends on the
benchmark gap, so there are no arbitrary salary cliffs.
</p>

<p>
That player may still be worth every Coin. But under a 500-Coin Salary Cap,
the rest of the franchise has to live with that decision too.
</p>

<p>
Keeping an elite player is easy. Building around an increasingly expensive
elite player is the challenge.
</p>

<h2>The Rookie</h2>

<p>
Then there are players who enter the system differently.
</p>

<p>
Every IceCore Entry Draft selection joins his franchise at:
</p>

<div class="ic-article-flow">
  <strong>1 COIN</strong>
</div>

<p>
There is no immediate salary escalation.
</p>

<p>
He was drafted at 1 Coin.
</p>

<p>
That is his salary.
</p>

<p>
If he eventually becomes a regular player and is protected as a Regular Keeper
in a future offseason, the normal salary escalation rules begin.
</p>

<p>
Imagine that rookie becomes eligible for the IceCore Value Bonus before his
next regular keeper decision.
</p>

<div class="ic-article-flow">
  <span>1</span>
  <span>→</span>
  <strong>10</strong>
</div>

<p>
The franchise receives the benefit of finding and developing the player.
</p>

<p>
Success eventually begins moving the contract.
</p>

<h2>The Prospect Who Stays on the Farm</h2>

<p>
Development Keepers work differently from Regular Keepers.
</p>

<p>
A Development Keeper retains the IceCore salary at which the player was
acquired. If the player was acquired through Free Agency or Waivers, his
salary is 0 Coins. If he was acquired through the Salary Cap Draft, his
salary is the winning bid.
</p>

<p>
A Development Keeper receives a separate <strong>+1 Coin increase for each offseason in which he is protected again as a Development Keeper</strong>.
</p>

<p>
The development system is there to reward scouting and patience without
creating an arbitrary salary progression before the player becomes part of
the regular roster economy.
</p>

<p>
When that player eventually becomes a regular keeper, the normal salary
escalation rules begin to matter.
</p>

<h2>Cheap Does Not Mean Permanent</h2>

<p>
This is the balance IceCore is trying to create.
</p>

<p>
Find a breakout early?
</p>

<p>
You deserve the advantage.
</p>

<p>
Develop a prospect for years?
</p>

<p>
You deserve the 1-Coin asset.
</p>

<p>
Discover somebody on waivers before the rest of the league?
</p>

<p>
You deserve the bargain.
</p>

<p>
But once those players become established pieces of the franchise,
their contracts begin moving with them.
</p>

<p>
The system does not immediately erase good scouting by forcing every
breakout player to market value.
</p>

<p>
And it does not allow elite production to remain almost free forever.
</p>

<blockquote>
Find value early.<br>
Benefit from it.<br>
Then decide how long it is still worth keeping.
</blockquote>

<h2>The Decision Returns Every Year</h2>

<p>
That is what salary escalation is really for.
</p>

<p>
Not inflation for the sake of inflation.
</p>

<p>
Not punishment for finding a great player.
</p>

<p>
It makes the same question return every offseason:
</p>

<blockquote>
At his new salary, do I still want him?
</blockquote>

<p>
Sometimes the answer will be obvious.
</p>

<p>
Sometimes the 4-Coin increase will be enough to change it. Sometimes the
additional Value Bonus will make the decision even harder.
</p>

<p>
Sometimes a 100-Coin superstar will still be worth every bit of the cap
space he consumes.
</p>

<p>
And sometimes the hardest decision will be releasing a player you found
years ago for almost nothing.
</p>

<p>
That is not the system taking your advantage away.
</p>

<p>
That is the advantage becoming a contract.
</p>

<p>
<strong>Great value should be rewarded.</strong>
</p>

<p>
<strong>Great value should also evolve.</strong>
</p>
