## 🧊 Cleveland Forge 850 — 667 Saskatoon Prairie Drifters

### A record-setting week — and a poll upset.

Cleveland delivered the week's headline performance, piling up **850 fantasy points**, while Saskatoon finished on 667. Yahoo's original projection was **644–602**, so Cleveland blew past its projected total by more than 200 points.

The Forge's production came across **64 player-games**, working out to **13.28 fantasy points per player-game**. That makes the record even more impressive: it was not simply a case of having a particularly busy schedule.

**Sam Rinzel (97)** and **Robert Thomas (94)** led the charge, with **Roman Kantserov (70)** also making a major contribution. On Saskatoon's side, **Patrick Kane (84)** was the standout, while Filip Hronek added 49.

The poll had strongly backed Saskatoon, so Cleveland's win was one of the round's biggest prediction misses.

**Prediction: ❌ Saskatoon**

**Story of the matchup:** Cleveland not only won — it established a new scoring benchmark for IceCore.

## 🥃 Key West Rumrunners 528 — 621 Quebec City Citadels

### Quebec delivered, with Eriksson Ek and Schaefer leading the way.

Yahoo projected a close matchup, **525–591** in Quebec's favour. The Citadels followed that script, winning **621–528**.

**Joel Eriksson Ek (71)** was Quebec's leading scorer, with **Matthew Schaefer (68)** and Alex Tuch (59) also producing strong weeks. Jack Quinn chipped in 58.

Key West had a solid contribution from **Zach Werenski (66)**. Elias Lindholm (55) and Logan Stankoven (53) also had productive outings, but Quebec's depth across the lineup was enough to maintain the advantage.

**Prediction: ✅ Quebec**

**Story of the matchup:** Quebec's balanced production backed up the projection, with Schaefer adding another eye-catching performance.

## 🟢 Green Bay Alliance 754 — 491 Kelowna Vintners

### Green Bay turned the projection upside down.

Yahoo expected Kelowna to win, projecting **629–575**. Instead, Green Bay put together one of the round's strongest performances and won by **263 points**.

**Jake Neighbours (96)** was the standout, while **Logan Mailloux (85)** and Philip Broberg (67) delivered a huge contribution from the blue line. Mikko Rantanen added 61.

Kelowna's best effort came from **Jared Spurgeon (53)**, with Darren Raddysh close behind at 52 and Kent Johnson contributing 44. But the Vintners could not match Green Bay's scoring spread.

Connor Bedard is listed with an injury designation in the matchup export and contributed 10 fantasy points; Green Bay's big week was nevertheless led by Neighbours, Mailloux and Broberg.

**Prediction: ❌ Kelowna**

**Story of the matchup:** Neighbours and Mailloux led a powerful Green Bay performance that completely overturned Yahoo's pre-match expectation.

# 🧭 THE ICECORE OFFSEASON — ONE QUESTION TO SETTLE FIRST

IceCore is still in its first season, but in many ways this is also a learning season — a chance to test Yahoo's capabilities, refine the league's processes and build a dynasty format that is both comprehensive and reasonably close to the NHL model.

**There will be no rule changes before the final IceCore matchup.** The priority is to settle the farm-keeper rules early enough for managers to make their keeper decisions correctly after the final game of the season.

### 🌱 The farm-keeper proposal

The current limit of **3 prospects + 1 rookie** was built around the expectation that teams would use the four weekly adds to move players to the farm after the draft, with those adds then available for waiver pickups during the season.

Yahoo surprised us by allowing unlimited adds before Matchup 1. That means managers can move more players to their farm before the season begins — and the IceCore website was designed with the possibility of protecting the entire farm in mind.

The proposal is therefore:

- Keep **the entire farm + one rookie**, up to a maximum of **six protected players**.
- A young player brought up from the AHL and added to an NHL active roster late in the season could also qualify if he has played fewer than **15 NHL regular-season games**.
- Protected players would remain associated with their team, be displayed on the IceCore website, and could be reclaimed after the preseason draft by moving them from waivers to the farm or roster.
- Teams must reclaim protected players by **Matchup 1**. If they do not, the protection rights expire.
- If another manager mistakenly claims a protected player, the player would be removed from that roster, with no coins refunded.
- Protected players would not occupy regular roster slots during the preseason draft.

This would give the farm a more meaningful role, better separate prospects from the active roster, and more closely resemble NHL-style prospect management.

Yahoo does not allow us to keep farm players directly in NA slots, so this protection system would be a practical workaround. Protected players would be listed in team cards on the IceCore website and included in the pre-draft summary.

### 🏒 Rookie draft idea

We could also make the rookie selection more engaging by running an **offline snake draft in the league chat after the real NHL Rookie Draft**. This is only an idea for discussion, but it could add another event to the offseason.

### ☀️ Other offseason topics

These are not changes for now. We will review them in the summer:

- In-season salary cap — setting the exact amount.
- LTIR salary relief, following the NHL model.
- Possible increase in IR+ slots.
- Minimum salary of 1 coin.
- A technical BN slot, used strictly for trades involving injured or NA players. After the trade, the player would have to be moved out of BN immediately, leaving the slot empty during matchups. This is an option for discussion, not a settled rule.

**Scoring will remain unchanged.** The current system is unique, balanced across positions and has received positive feedback from managers.
