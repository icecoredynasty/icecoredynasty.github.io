# ICECORE 2.0 – A LEAGUE THAT LISTENS

### Proposed changes, new ideas and the future direction of IceCore Dynasty

IceCore Dynasty has always been built around competition, strategy, long-term team building and, above all, the people who make this league what it is.

As commissioners, we have been paying close attention to recent discussions, suggestions and requests from our GMs. Some of the changes presented below were not originally part of our plans at all. However, a successful dynasty league cannot be built solely around the ideas of its commissioners. It must evolve together with its members.

**IceCore listens. IceCore wants to be successful in the long run.** Our goal is to introduce improvements that can increase the attractiveness, balance and fairness of the league while preserving its identity and competitive spirit.

We also understand that some requests concern matters that do not necessarily need to wait until the end of the season. That is why we are raising an important question:

**Would GMs be willing to accept selected changes as early as Gameweek 8?**

Below, we present the proposals we would like to discuss together.

---

## 1. In-season Salary Cap – maximum 550 coins

The proposed in-season salary cap would be set at **550 coins**, representing a maximum increase of 10% compared to the original draft cap of 500 coins.

Since no team currently exceeds this limit, we do not expect any immediate disruption to the competition or the need for major roster adjustments.

Commissioners would carry out random and ongoing salary cap checks. If a team is found to be over the limit, the GM would receive a warning and have **one week to make the necessary adjustments**.

The goal is not to punish teams, but to establish clear and consistent financial rules throughout the season.

## 2. Salary Cap Exemptions – IR-LT and NA Farm

Players placed on IR-LT would not count towards the in-season salary cap.

Players on the NA farm would also receive salary relief based on a simplified NHL-style principle:

- A player with a salary of 1 coin would count as 0 coins against the cap.
- A player with a salary of 5 coins would count as 4 coins against the cap.
- In general, a player's salary would be reduced by 1 coin for cap purposes while on NA, but never below zero.

The farm system would therefore provide more room for developing young players without unnecessary pressure on the salary cap.

## 3. Minimum Salary – 1 coin

A minimum salary of **1 coin for every player** would be introduced.

This rule would apply to:

- New players acquired through waivers.
- Players placed on the NA farm.
- Any player entering the league without an existing IC salary.

The goal is to eliminate the acquisition of players at zero salary and maintain a consistent salary structure throughout the league.

## 4. Salary Floor – 300 coins

The proposed minimum team salary, or salary floor, would be adjusted to **300 coins**.

This would bring the minimum team spending closer to the financial balance used in the NHL and support the competitiveness of all franchises.

The idea is not to force GMs to spend recklessly, but to prevent teams from operating significantly below the financial level needed to remain competitive over the long term.

## 5. One Additional BN Slot – Flexible Bench Spot

We propose adding **one BN slot** to every team.

This will be a fully flexible roster spot, giving GMs additional options when managing their teams. It can be used to hold a healthy player outside the active lineup, temporarily manage roster space after a trade, or simply provide more flexibility during the season.

The salary of a player placed in BN will continue to count fully against the salary cap.

The additional BN slot will not increase the active roster size, which will remain at 20 players.

This addition will also help address a Yahoo Fantasy limitation. When a player who was placed in IR+ on his previous team is acquired through a trade, Yahoo may automatically assign him to the active roster of the receiving team instead of IR+. The additional BN spot will give GMs more room to manage situations like this. However, players acquired from NA or IR+ through a trade will not automatically be placed in BN.

## 6. One Additional IR+ Slot

The number of IR+ slots would increase from **3 to 4**.

GMs would gain a little more room to manage injuries and their rosters without being forced to drop players unnecessarily.

## 7. Rookie Picks – One Player, One Day

The rookie pick system remains unchanged in principle, but the selection process would be adjusted.

The day after the real NHL Draft, each team would have exactly **one day to select one player**.

If a GM does not make a selection within the designated time, the commissioners will automatically assign the highest available player selected in the real NHL Draft.

The rookie player will not occupy an active roster spot during the fantasy draft. Each GM will hold separate rights to this player.

Every franchise will therefore have a fair opportunity to secure its rookie without interfering with the regular draft process.

## 8. Protected Farm Rights – Up to Five Farm Players

We propose a clearer and more flexible approach to the NA farm system.

Each GM will be able to protect up to **five farm players** at the end of the season. Together with the rookie pick, a franchise may therefore hold rights to a maximum of six players outside the regular draft selection.

These rights will be recorded and published by the league.

If another team selects or claims a protected player during the draft, the commissioners will remove the player and return him to waivers. The original GM will retain the rights to that player.

After the draft, GMs will have a designated one-week period with unlimited free adds to reclaim their protected players:

- Players with Yahoo NA status will be placed on the NA farm.
- Players without NA status will have to be placed on the active roster.

If a GM does not reclaim a player by the first game of the first week of the regular season, their rights to that player will expire and the player will become available to everyone.

An exception applies when a protected player is not yet listed in Yahoo. In that case, the GM does not lose their rights. Once the player becomes available in Yahoo, the GM can decide whether to place him on the farm or active roster, or relinquish the rights.

We also propose a safeguard for young players who lose their Yahoo NA status during the season. A GM could still protect them as farm players as long as they have played fewer than 15 NHL games.

The goal is to protect the long-term development of players while maintaining a simple system without unnecessary restrictions during the regular draft.

## 9. Salary Benchmarks – PAVA System

We also propose introducing the **PAVA system** for determining salary benchmarks.

Its purpose is to provide fairer and smoother salary reductions between individual players and benchmark levels.

We do not want to overcomplicate things here. The intention is simple: to achieve a more consistent and balanced salary evaluation system.

## 10. Floating Base – Greater Flexibility

We propose expanding the Floating Base range to **+2 to +6**.

The Floating Base will continue to respond to changes in salary benchmarks. If benchmarks rise significantly, the Base may decrease. If benchmarks fall, it may increase. However, any adjustment will be limited to a maximum of one point per season.

Importantly, the Base will not be determined by the commissioners alone. We want GMs to participate in this process and have a say in how the league's salary system evolves.

The purpose is to maintain a long-term balance between player value, salaries and team budgets, while keeping salary inflation under control.

## 11. Scoring Adjustments – Starting Next Season

Scoring cannot be changed during the current season. However, we would like to present a proposal for next season.

Proposed additions:

- **PIM:** −1 point for every penalty minute.
- **+/−:** +1 point for every plus, −1 point for every minus.
- **FW:** +1 point for every faceoff won.
- **FL:** −1 point for every faceoff lost.
- **Goalie Loss (L):** −1 point for every goalie loss.

All existing IC scoring values will remain unchanged.

We have already tested these additions and the overall impact on scoring was approximately **+3.5%**.

The results suggest that the new categories could bring more hockey context into the scoring system and reward additional aspects of player performance without fundamentally changing the existing scoring structure.

These changes would take effect next season only.

## 12. Trade and Waiver Press Statements

We would also like to introduce a small community feature inspired by other fantasy leagues.

With every completed trade or waiver add, GMs would have the option to include **a one-sentence statement** explaining the reason or objective behind their move.

For example:

- What motivated this trade?
- What does the new player bring to the team?
- Is this a short-term move or part of a long-term strategy?

This is not intended to create additional administration or force GMs to justify every decision. It is simply a way to bring more personality, context and media activity into IceCore.

## 13. Activity, Respect and League Standards

IceCore wants to be an active, competitive and respectful community.

Our goal is for everyone to feel welcome, regardless of their position in the standings. Rivalry and healthy competitive banter are welcome, but personal insults and disrespect towards other GMs are not.

A warning has already been issued for insulting league opponents. Commissioners will not hesitate to replace a GM who repeatedly fails to control their behaviour and disrupts the community.

At the same time, we understand that some GMs are currently busy or have limited time. We communicate with them and try to find the best possible solutions for individuals and the league as a whole.

We also recognize the value of experienced fantasy managers. Their knowledge, strategic awareness and long-term approach are important assets to IceCore. We want to build an environment where experienced GMs can contribute to the league's development and where knowledge and ideas can be shared across the community.

Commissioners can see login activity and identify rosters that have not been updated despite injured players or other obvious situations requiring attention.

However, long-term inactivity that negatively affects a franchise's results cannot be ignored. GMs whose repeated inactivity damages the competitiveness of their own team may be removed from their managerial position and replaced.

Every franchise deserves an active manager who takes responsibility for its future.

We also want IceCore 2.0 to be built together with its GMs. Commissioners will personally contact every manager to collect feedback, discuss the proposals and understand what works well and what could be improved.

Yahoo Fantasy has its technical limitations, but we will do our best to make the most informed decisions possible with the tools available to us, so that IceCore can remain a high-quality, successful league in the long term.

# THE BIG QUESTION – GW8 OR NEXT SEASON?

Not all proposals need to be introduced immediately. Some changes, especially those concerning goalie keepers and scoring, naturally belong to the next offseason.

However, the in-season salary cap and selected roster management changes raise an important question: **should we introduce them as early as GW8, or wait until the end of the season?**

Our current proposal is to introduce the agreed changes from GW8, with two exceptions:

- **Goalie Keepers:** The option to protect one or two goalies would be introduced next season. The maximum keeper limit remains 15.
- **Scoring:** All proposed scoring changes would take effect next season only.

Before making a final decision, we want to hear your opinions.

These are proposals, not final decisions. We are presenting them because we believe IceCore should evolve through discussion, not through decisions made behind closed doors.

**IceCore is your league too. We listen, we adapt, and we want to build something together that remains competitive, fair and fun for many years to come.**

Let's discuss.
