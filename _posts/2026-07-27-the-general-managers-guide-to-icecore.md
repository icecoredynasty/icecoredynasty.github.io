Taking control of an IceCore franchise is simple.

Building a great one isn't.

IceCore Dynasty combines long-term roster building, player salaries, prospects, drafts and franchise history without turning fantasy hockey into a second job.

You don't need to learn everything on day one.

You need to think like a General Manager.

## Your Franchise

An IceCore franchise is more than the roster you happen to own this season.

Its identity stays.

Its history stays.

Its championships, records, rivalries and defining decisions become part of the league.

General Managers may change.

The franchise continues.

Your job is to decide what the next chapter looks like.

## Build the Team

Every franchise builds around a 20-player active roster.

There is no fantasy bench.

If you signed a player, he is healthy and his NHL team puts him on the ice, his performance counts for your franchise.

That changes the job.

You are not deciding which of your own players should be allowed to score fantasy points tonight.

You are deciding which players deserve to belong to your organization.

**IceCore is a GM game, not a coaching game.**

## Manage 500 Coins

Every franchise operates under the same 500 Coin Salary Cap.

Coins represent fantasy player value rather than real-world NHL contracts.

Some players will become bargains.

Some contracts will become uncomfortable.

A breakout player acquired cheaply can become one of the most valuable assets in the league.

A player you believed in may become an expensive mistake.

Salary is not decoration.

It is part of roster construction.

## Keep Your Core

Dynasty begins when the season ends.

Each franchise may carry a substantial part of its roster into the following year, allowing successful cores to stay together while still forcing meaningful offseason decisions.

You won't automatically keep everyone.

Cap space, player value, age, development and future plans all matter.

A contender may protect most of its established roster.

A rebuilding franchise may deliberately carry fewer players forward and enter the market with more flexibility.

Both can be legitimate strategies.

## Develop the Future

IceCore gives every franchise room to hold young players outside the active roster through NA positions.

You can ignore deep prospect hunting and keep the system simple.

Or you can build for years ahead.

The choice is yours.

At least one NA player carries forward with the franchise each offseason, ensuring that development remains part of every organization without requiring a separate prospect database.

## Draft One Rookie

Every offseason brings one new first-round NHL prospect into each IceCore organization through the Entry Draft.

The order is determined by reverse standings.

There is no lottery.

There are no traded Entry Draft selections.

The weakest franchise gets the first opportunity to add the next generation of talent.

Every franchise gets one.

## Enter the Market

After keepers, prospects and rookies are established, the remaining roster positions are filled through the Salary Cap Draft.

This is where different franchise strategies collide.

One GM may arrive with a nearly complete roster and little cap room.

Another may enter with several vacancies and hundreds of Coins available.

Same cap.

Different decisions.

Different teams.

## Use Waivers

The draft does not finish the job.

Players emerge.

Roles change.

Injuries happen.

Prospects arrive.

Mistakes become obvious.

Waivers and FAAB give every General Manager another way to improve the organization throughout the season.

The best move of your year may happen long after draft night.

## Trade

Players can move.

Their salary moves with them.

That means a trade is not only about which player is better.

Age, salary, roster construction, competitive window and future value can all change what an asset is worth to a franchise.

A great trade should make sense for both organizations for different reasons.

## Choose Your Direction

There is no required way to build an IceCore franchise.

Compete now.

Build around youth.

Find undervalued veterans.

Invest in prospects.

Create cap flexibility.

Keep a large core.

Rebuild aggressively.

The systems provide the tools.

The decisions belong to you.

## Start Simple

You do not need dynasty experience to manage an IceCore franchise.

Know your players.

Stay active.

Meet league deadlines.

Make decisions.

The deeper systems become useful when you need them.

**Build the roster. Manage the future. Let the players play.**
