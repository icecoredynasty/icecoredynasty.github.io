Three gameweeks are now in the books, and IceCore Dynasty is beginning to take shape on the ice as well as off it.

With the first weeks of competition behind us, we have some early standout performances, a clearer picture of which players are outperforming expectations, and an updated Value Board to help General Managers plan ahead. There are also a couple of league updates to share.

## League Updates: A New Co-Commissioner and Trade Vetoes

For anyone who may have missed the discussion on Reddit, **Murad has joined IceCore as our new Co-Commissioner**.

We have also introduced a new trade-veto system. From now on, **a trade requires seven GM veto votes to be blocked**. The aim is to give General Managers a greater role in trade oversight while keeping the process straightforward.

## The Tkachuk Brothers Set the Pace

The standout individual performances of GW3 came from the Tkachuk brothers.

**Matthew Tkachuk scored 104 points, while Brady Tkachuk reached 121 points**, setting a new league record for the time being.

## Value Board Updated: A First Look at Salary Growth

The **[IceCore Dynasty Value Board](https://icecoredynasty.com/value-board/)** has been updated to show how salary bonuses would look if the season ended after GW3.

The board gives GMs an early indication of potential salary increases based on player value. It can help with salary-cap planning and keeper decisions, particularly when assessing which contracts could become more expensive as the season develops.

The figures are **provisional and may contain errors**. The board will be updated periodically, and the final table will go through a two-step verification process. For now, it should be treated as a planning tool, not a final salary calculation.

## Looking Ahead

Three gameweeks provide the first useful data points, but there is still a long season ahead. Player rankings, roster performance and projected salary changes will continue to evolve.

For now, the main takeaways are clear: Murad has joined the league's leadership team, the trade-veto process has been updated, the Tkachuk brothers have delivered the week's headline performances, and the Value Board is available for GMs to review.

We will continue tracking league developments and updating the relevant information as the season progresses.
