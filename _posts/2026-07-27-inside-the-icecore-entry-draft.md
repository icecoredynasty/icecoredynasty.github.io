Every dynasty needs a future.

In IceCore, that future enters the league one player at a time.

Every offseason, each franchise receives one selection in the **IceCore Entry Draft**.

Twenty franchises.

Twenty selections.

Twenty new players entering organizations that may shape their careers for years.

## One Rookie Per Franchise

The Entry Draft is deliberately small.

IceCore does not need a five-round prospect draft followed by dozens of rights held in an external spreadsheet.

Every franchise selects exactly one eligible rookie.

That player enters the organization at a salary of **1 Coin**.

Simple enough for every General Manager to follow.

Important enough to change a franchise.

## Competitive Balance Comes First

The Entry Draft uses reverse standings.

The lowest-ranked franchise selects first.

The champion selects last.

There is no lottery.

Draft selections cannot be traded.

That is intentional.

The Entry Draft is one of IceCore's strongest tools for competitive balance.

A struggling franchise should know that surviving a difficult season leaves it with the first opportunity to add the next generation of talent.

## Why Picks Stay Home

Trading draft selections creates strategy.

It can also create problems that survive longer than the General Manager who made the trade.

IceCore franchises are permanent.

Their managers may not be.

Protecting Entry Draft selections ensures that every organization enters every offseason with an opportunity to improve.

No franchise can inherit years of missing first-round selections because of decisions made by somebody who no longer manages it.

The future always has a way back in.

## Prospects Without the Spreadsheet

IceCore supports long-term prospect management through its roster structure and NA positions rather than a separate database of player rights.

Managers who love scouting can invest deeply in young talent.

Managers who prefer a simpler experience do not need to maintain a farm system in another application.

The Entry Draft connects both approaches.

Everyone participates.

Everyone adds one rookie.

What happens next is up to the General Manager.

## The First Pick

Every Entry Draft will eventually become part of IceCore history.

Some first selections will become superstars.

Some late selections will become steals.

Some obvious choices will fail.

Some decisions will look completely different five years later.

That is dynasty hockey.

The draft takes minutes.

**Its consequences can last for years.**
