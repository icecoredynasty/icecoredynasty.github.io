  <section class="ic-awards-section">

    <header class="ic-awards-heading">
      <span>GENERAL MANAGERS</span>
      <h2>Management Awards</h2>

      <p>
        Championships tell part of the story.
        These awards recognize the decisions behind them.
      </p>
    </header>


    <div class="ic-award-list">

      <article>

        <span class="ic-award-list__number">01</span>

        <div>

          <h3>GM of the Year</h3>
          <strong>Outstanding General Manager</strong>

          <p>
            The league's premier management award, recognizing competitive
            success, roster construction, transactions, draft decisions and
            overall franchise management.
          </p>

        </div>

      </article>


      <article>

        <span class="ic-award-list__number">02</span>

        <div>

          <h3>Architect Award</h3>
          <strong>Best Franchise Building</strong>

          <p>
            Recognizes outstanding long-term roster construction:
            talent, salary value, development depth and future flexibility.
          </p>

        </div>

      </article>


      <article>

        <span class="ic-award-list__number">03</span>

        <div>

          <h3>Turnaround Award</h3>
          <strong>Most Improved Franchise</strong>

          <p>
            Awarded for the most impressive transformation from the previous
            season through roster development and management.
          </p>

        </div>

      </article>


      <article>

        <span class="ic-award-list__number">04</span>

        <div>

          <h3>Draft Master</h3>
          <strong>Best Draft Performance</strong>

          <p>
            Recognizes the GM who created the greatest value through the
            Entry Draft and Live Salary Cap Draft.
          </p>

        </div>

      </article>


      <article>

        <span class="ic-award-list__number">05</span>

        <div>

          <h3>Iron GM</h3>
          <strong>Franchise Stewardship</strong>

          <p>
            Recognition for exceptional reliability, participation
            and franchise stewardship throughout the entire season.
            A long-term award for the GM who can be trusted to keep
            a franchise active and competitive.
          </p>

        </div>

      </article>


      <article>

        <span class="ic-award-list__number">06</span>

        <div>

          <h3>Most Active GM</h3>
          <strong>League Participation Award</strong>

          <p>
            Awarded to the GM who best combines sustained league participation,
            communication and activity throughout the season. The award is
            voted on from the GMs with the strongest activity-star records,
            with Yahoo participation statistics and league records used as
            supporting evidence where available.
          </p>

        </div>

      </article>

    </div>

  </section>

  <!-- SEASON MOMENTS -->

  <section class="ic-awards-section">

    <header class="ic-awards-heading">

      <span>SEASON IN REVIEW</span>
      <h2>Moments & Records</h2>

      <p>
        Not every memorable performance needs a trophy.
        Some simply become part of league history.
      </p>

    </header>


    <div class="ic-move-grid">

      <article>

        <span>PLAYER</span>
        <h3>Monster Night</h3>

        <p>
          The highest single-game fantasy score recorded by a skater
          during the IceCore season.
        </p>

      </article>


      <article>

        <span>GOALTENDER</span>
        <h3>Brick Wall</h3>

        <p>
          The highest single-game fantasy score recorded by a goaltender,
          where saves, wins and the 13-point shutout bonus can create
          one of the season's defining performances.
        </p>

      </article>


      <article>

        <span>GOALTENDER</span>
        <h3>Meltdown</h3>

        <p>
          The lowest single-game fantasy score recorded by a goaltender.
          With every goal against worth −3, the wrong night can become
          just as memorable as the right one.
        </p>

      </article>


      <article>

        <span>MATCHUP</span>
        <h3>Photo Finish</h3>

        <p>
          The closest completed matchup of the season,
          recognizing the week decided by the smallest final margin.
        </p>

      </article>


      <article>

        <span>MATCHUP</span>
        <h3>Statement Win</h3>

        <p>
          The largest winning margin recorded in a completed
          head-to-head matchup during the season.
        </p>

      </article>


      <article>

        <span>WAIVERS</span>
        <h3>Waiver Hero</h3>

        <p>
          A memorable performance from a player acquired through waivers
          shortly before delivering a matchup-changing result.
        </p>

      </article>


      <article>

        <span>REGULAR SEASON</span>
        <h3>Points Machine</h3>

        <p>
          The franchise that accumulates the highest total fantasy points
          across the regular season.
        </p>

      </article>


      <article>

        <span>STREAK</span>
        <h3>Run of Form</h3>

        <p>
          The longest winning streak recorded by a franchise during
          the regular season.
        </p>

      </article>


      <article>

        <span>RECORD</span>
        <h3>Season Record</h3>

        <p>
          A permanent archive of league records such as highest team score,
          highest player score, largest margin, closest matchup and other
          measurable Yahoo statistics.
        </p>

      </article>

    </div>

  </section>
