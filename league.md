  <!-- GM NOT COACH -->
  <section class="league-feature league-feature--soft">

    <div class="league-feature__header">
      <span class="league-eyebrow">THE GENERAL MANAGER</span>
      <span class="league-number">03</span>
    </div>

    <div class="league-philosophy">

      <h2>
        Your job is to build the team.<br>
        Not coach Saturday night.
      </h2>

      <blockquote>
        “You don't choose when your players count.
        You choose which players are worth counting on.”
      </blockquote>

      <p>
        Signed a player? If he is eligible, healthy and his NHL team
        puts him in the game, his performance belongs to your franchise.
      </p>

      <p>
        There is no reason to own a player and then decide that his
        real NHL performance should disappear because he happened
        to sit on a fantasy bench.
      </p>

    </div>

  </section>

        Deep enough to matter.<br>
        Simple enough to enjoy.
      </h2>

        occupying every day of the offseason or requiring constant
        lineup maintenance during the season.
      </p>

      <p>

        and removes complexity that exists only for complexity's sake.
      </p>

        be allowed to score tonight.”
