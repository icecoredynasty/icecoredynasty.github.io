        <p>
          IceCore uses a Head-to-Head Points system built around the
          <strong>six consecutive Fibonacci values</strong>:
          <strong>1 · 2 · 3 · 5 · 8 · 13</strong>.
        </p>

        <p>
          The sequence creates the scoring architecture of IceCore.
          Every category is assigned a value according to its importance,
          frequency or rarity — from the smallest contributions at 1
          to the biggest single-event reward at 13.
        </p>

        <p>
          Shots, hits, saves and power-play points form the foundation at
          <strong>1</strong>. Blocks rise to <strong>2</strong>.
          Goals against carry a <strong>−3</strong> penalty.
          Short-handed points and wins are worth <strong>5</strong>.
          Goals and assists sit at <strong>8</strong>.
          A shutout reaches <strong>13</strong>.
        </p>

        <p>
          The numbers are fixed. The hockey categories determine where
          they belong. The result is a scoring system that is simple to
          understand, difficult to exploit and unmistakably IceCore.
        </p>
