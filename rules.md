    <div class="rules-section__content">
      <span class="rules-eyebrow">ROSTER</span>
      <h2>Twenty players. No bench.</h2>

      <p>
        Each franchise builds a twenty-player NHL roster.
        Every eligible rostered player contributes whenever
        he plays for his NHL team.
      </p>

      <div class="rules-roster">

        <div>
          <strong>4</strong>
          <span>Centers</span>
        </div>

        <div>
          <strong>4</strong>
          <span>Left Wings</span>
        </div>

        <div>
          <strong>4</strong>
          <span>Right Wings</span>
        </div>

        <div>
          <strong>6</strong>
          <span>Defensemen</span>
        </div>

        <div>
          <strong>2</strong>
          <span>Goaltenders</span>
        </div>

      </div>

      <div class="rules-note">
        <strong>Additional roster:</strong>
        IR+ slots provide injury relief and NA slots provide
        development space for eligible prospects. Exact platform
        settings are always shown in the official Yahoo league settings.
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>


  <section class="rules-section" id="scoring">
    <div class="rules-section__number">02</div>

    <div class="rules-section__content">
      <span class="rules-eyebrow">SCORING</span>
      <h2>Every performance counts.</h2>

      <p>
        IceCore uses Head-to-Head Points scoring.
        The scoring model rewards offensive production,
        shooting, physical play, defensive contribution
        and individual goaltending performance.
      </p>

      <p>
        There are no bench decisions. If an eligible player
        on your roster plays, his performance counts toward
        the weekly matchup.
      </p>

      <div class="rules-note">
        The official scoring values are published in the Yahoo
        league settings and apply equally to every franchise.
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>


  <section class="rules-section" id="salary">
    <div class="rules-section__number">03</div>

    <div class="rules-section__content">
      <span class="rules-eyebrow">SALARY</span>
      <h2>500 Coins. Spend them wisely.</h2>

      <p>
        Every franchise operates under a
        <strong>500 Coin Salary Cap</strong>.
        Player salaries established through the draft or keeper
        process remain attached to that player during the season,
        including after a trade or release and subsequent acquisition.
      </p>

      <p>
        Players acquired from free agency or waivers who did not
        previously carry an IceCore salary that season enter with
        a salary basis of <strong>0 Coins</strong>.
      </p>

      <div class="rules-note">
        FAAB and player salary are separate systems.
        A waiver bid does not become the player's IceCore salary.
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>


  <section class="rules-section" id="keepers">
    <div class="rules-section__number">04</div>

    <div class="rules-section__content">
      <span class="rules-eyebrow">KEEPERS</span>
      <h2>Choose what survives the offseason.</h2>

      <p>
        Each franchise protects between
        <strong>12 and 16 regular keepers</strong>
        before the annual keeper deadline.
      </p>

      <p>
        Keeper salaries increase each offseason according to
        the player's season-ending ranking and current salary.
        The applicable increase is calculated before the next draft.
      </p>

      <div class="rules-note">
        Franchises may protect different numbers of players.
        Keeping fewer players creates more roster openings and
        additional flexibility in the Salary Cap Draft.
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>


  <section class="rules-section" id="prospects">
    <div class="rules-section__number">05</div>

    <div class="rules-section__content">
      <span class="rules-eyebrow">PROSPECTS</span>
      <h2>Build beyond today.</h2>

      <p>
        Each franchise carries one designated NA keeper into
        the offseason process. Additional eligible prospects may
        be developed through the league's NA roster slots during
        the season.
      </p>

      <p>
        An NA keeper carries a salary of
        <strong>1 Coin</strong>.
        Additional NA players used among regular keepers also
        carry the applicable prospect salary defined by league rules.
      </p>

      <div class="rules-note">
        NA eligibility itself is determined by Yahoo.
        Managers are responsible for maintaining a legal roster
        when player eligibility changes.
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>


  <section class="rules-section" id="entry-draft">
    <div class="rules-section__number">06</div>

    <div class="rules-section__content">
      <span class="rules-eyebrow">ENTRY DRAFT</span>
      <h2>One franchise. One rookie.</h2>

      <p>
        Every offseason, each franchise selects
        <strong>one rookie</strong> through the IceCore Entry Draft.
      </p>

      <p>
        Draft order follows the reverse order of the previous
        season standings. There is no lottery and draft selections
        cannot be traded.
      </p>

      <p>
        The selected rookie enters the franchise at
        <strong>1 Coin</strong> and is assigned to the team before
        the Yahoo Salary Cap Draft.
      </p>

      <div class="rules-note">
        One rookie per franchise preserves competitive balance
        and guarantees every organization access to the next
        generation of players.
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>


  <section class="rules-section" id="salary-draft">
    <div class="rules-section__number">07</div>

    <div class="rules-section__content">
      <span class="rules-eyebrow">SALARY CAP DRAFT</span>
      <h2>Build the rest live.</h2>

      <p>
        After keepers, the designated NA player and the Entry Draft
        rookie are assigned, remaining roster positions are filled
        through the annual Yahoo Live Salary Cap Draft.
      </p>

      <p>
        Keeper salaries are deducted from each franchise's
        500 Coin budget before the live draft begins.
        Teams therefore enter the same draft with different
        remaining budgets and different roster needs.
      </p>

      <p>
        Depending on keeper strategy, franchises may enter the
        Salary Cap Draft with approximately
        <strong>2–6 roster positions</strong> to fill.
      </p>
    </div>
  </section>


  <section class="rules-section" id="free-agency">
    <div class="rules-section__number">08</div>

    <div class="rules-section__content">
      <span class="rules-eyebrow">WAIVERS & FREE AGENCY</span>
      <h2>The roster is never finished.</h2>

      <p>
        IceCore uses a seasonal
        <strong>500 FAAB</strong> budget for waiver claims.
        Managers decide how aggressively to pursue emerging players,
        injury replacements and unexpected opportunities.
      </p>

      <p>
        Players acquired from waivers or free agency without an
        existing IceCore salary begin with a salary basis of
        <strong>0 Coins</strong>.
        If protected the following offseason, the appropriate
        keeper increase is applied.
      </p>

      <p>
        A player who already carried an IceCore salary during that
        season retains that salary if released and later acquired
        by another franchise.
      </p>
    </div>
  </section>


  <section class="rules-section" id="trades">
    <div class="rules-section__number">09</div>

    <div class="rules-section__content">
      <span class="rules-eyebrow">TRADES</span>
      <h2>Players move. Contracts follow.</h2>

      <p>
        Trading is encouraged as a central part of franchise building.
        When a player is traded, his existing IceCore salary moves
        with him to the acquiring franchise.
      </p>

      <p>
        Entry Draft selections cannot be traded.
        All negotiations must be conducted in good faith.
      </p>

      <p>
        Commissioner intervention is reserved for collusion,
        exploitation or exceptional situations that threaten
        league integrity.
      </p>
    </div>
  </section>


  <section class="rules-section" id="activity">
    <div class="rules-section__number">10</div>

    <div class="rules-section__content">
      <span class="rules-eyebrow">GM ACTIVITY</span>
      <h2>Active management. No busywork.</h2>

      <p>
        IceCore expects active General Managers, but activity is
        measured through franchise management rather than daily
        lineup micromanagement.
      </p>

      <p>
        Managers are expected to meet league deadlines,
        participate in drafts, maintain legal rosters,
        respond to significant roster situations and remain
        reasonably available for league communication.
      </p>

      <p>
        Missing required deadlines may result in a warning.
        Repeated inactivity may lead to replacement of the General Manager.
      </p>
    </div>
  </section>


  <section class="rules-section" id="deadlines">
    <div class="rules-section__number">11</div>

    <div class="rules-section__content">
      <span class="rules-eyebrow">DEADLINES</span>
      <h2>The franchise keeps moving.</h2>

      <p>
        Keeper selections, Entry Draft preferences and other
        required offseason decisions must be submitted by the
        announced league deadlines.
      </p>

      <p>
        If a manager fails to submit keepers on time, the Commissioner
        may select the required players using objective league criteria,
        including player ranking, salary and roster eligibility.
      </p>

      <p>
        If a required Entry Draft preference list is not submitted,
        the Commissioner may use the real-world NHL first-round draft
        order as the default selection priority.
      </p>
    </div>
  </section>


  <section class="rules-section" id="orphan">
    <div class="rules-section__number">12</div>

    <div class="rules-section__content">
      <span class="rules-eyebrow">ORPHAN FRANCHISES</span>
      <h2>The franchise survives the GM.</h2>

      <p>
        IceCore franchises are permanent league identities.
        If a General Manager leaves, the franchise remains and
        ownership is transferred to a replacement GM.
      </p>

      <p>
        Until a replacement is appointed, the Commissioner may
        manage essential roster decisions to protect the competitive
        integrity and long-term health of the franchise.
      </p>
    </div>
  </section>


  <section class="rules-section" id="conduct">
    <div class="rules-section__number">13</div>

    <div class="rules-section__content">
      <span class="rules-eyebrow">LEAGUE CONDUCT</span>
      <h2>Compete hard. Run it well.</h2>

      <p>
        Rivalries, negotiation, disagreement and competitive
        pressure are part of IceCore.
        Personal attacks, harassment, collusion and deliberate
        attempts to damage the league are not.
      </p>

      <p>
        Commissioner decisions should prioritize consistency,
        competitive integrity and the long-term health of the league.
      </p>
    </div>
  </section>


  <section class="rules-footer">
    <span class="rules-eyebrow">THE STANDARD</span>
    <h2>Simple systems.<br>Meaningful decisions.</h2>

    <p>
      IceCore is designed to reward long-term franchise management
      without turning fantasy hockey into a second job.
      The rules create the framework. The General Managers create the history.
    </p>
