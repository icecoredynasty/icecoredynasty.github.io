How it works

Fan Points / Coin measures how efficiently a player converts salary into fantasy production.

The ranking is provisional during the Salary Cap Draft and changes as new players are drafted.

After the completed season, the final Value Board is used to determine the 60 Value Bonus players:

  • 20 players from ranks 1–50
  • 20 players from ranks 51–100
  • 20 players from ranks 101–150

Selection is based on Fan Points produced per Coin of salary during the completed season.
