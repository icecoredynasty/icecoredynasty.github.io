❓ FAQ & Calculation Guide

How is each group benchmark calculated?

Players are ranked by Yahoo Fan Points. The top 180 skaters are split into 18 groups of 10, and the top 20 goalies into two groups of 10. In each group, one highest salary and one lowest salary are excluded; the remaining eight salaries are averaged.

After calculating the raw group benchmarks, the Pool Adjacent Violators Algorithm (PAVA) is applied separately to skaters and goalies. It adjusts adjacent group values where necessary so that a lower-ranked group can never have a higher benchmark than the group above it. The displayed benchmark is then rounded to a whole Coin, with .5 rounded upward.

Why are the benchmarks shown only once per group?

The benchmark belongs to the entire ten-player performance group, not to an individual row. It is therefore shown once in the group heading rather than repeated for every player.

How is the projected Increase calculated?

Every managed player receives a +4 Base Increase. A player below the rounded benchmark receives an additional Value Bonus equal to one third of the positive gap.

Value Bonus = max(0, (Benchmark − Salary) ÷ 3)
Increase = 4 + Value Bonus

The Value Bonus and total Increase are rounded to a whole Coin, with .5 rounded upward. Players at or above the benchmark receive only +4. The official Base may later be +3, +4 or +5 under the league's Floating Base rule.

What does Value for Coin mean?

Value for Coin is a simple efficiency measure: Fan Points ÷ draft salary. It estimates how many Fan Points a player has produced per salary Coin in this snapshot. It is not part of the salary-escalation formula. To keep the comparison meaningful, it is displayed only for players with a salary above 2 Coins; others show a dash and are excluded from the Top 5 tables.

What happens when a player has no draft salary?

If a player is absent from Draft Results, the snapshot uses a salary of 0 Coins. That value remains part of the benchmark calculation and the player's Value Bonus is calculated normally if the player is assigned to a team in the Yahoo export. A zero salary alone does not prove that the player has no manager.

How does the Floating Base work?

The starting Base Increase is +4 Coins and can move only between +3 and +5. After the completed regular season, the league averages the 20 group benchmarks and rounds that average to the nearest whole Coin (.5 rounds upward). If the rounded average rises versus the previous season, the Base falls by 1; if it falls, the Base rises by 1; if unchanged, the Base stays the same. The Base never goes below +3 or above +5.

What is included in the total and average Value Bonus?

The total is the sum of the individual Value Bonuses for the 200 listed players. The average is that total divided by the number of listed players. The +4 Base Increase is deliberately excluded from both figures.

Can you show a simple example?

Suppose a player has a salary of 20 Coins and his group benchmark is 35 Coins. The positive gap is 15, so the Value Bonus is 15 ÷ 3 = 5 Coins. With the +4 Base Increase, his projected total Increase is +9 Coins.
