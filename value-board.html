💰 IceCore Value Board

Provisional Value Board · updated after Round X

The IceCore Value Board shows which players would currently qualify for the +5 IceCore Value Bonus under the league's salary escalation system. The board is informational during the season. Final bonus recipients are confirmed after the completed regular season.

Skaters are ranked by final Yahoo Fantasy Points and divided into groups of ten. The Top 10 goalies form one additional group. Within each group, the highest and lowest salary are removed and the remaining salaries are averaged to create that group's salary benchmark.

How to Read the Board

⚖️ Fair Salary Player's salary is at or above the current group benchmark. Base Increase only.
💎 IceCore Value Bonus Player's salary is below the current group benchmark and would receive the additional +5 bonus.
📊 Group Benchmark Average salary of the group after removing the highest- and lowest-paid player.

📌 Value Bonus Rules

Every regular keeper receives the +4 Base Increase. A player who finishes below the salary benchmark of his ranking group receives an additional +5 IceCore Value Bonus, for a maximum total increase of +9 Coins.

  • Skaters: final Yahoo Fantasy Point ranks 1–150, divided into groups of 10.
  • Goalies: final Yahoo Fantasy Point Top 10, treated as one group.
  • Each group benchmark is calculated after removing its highest and lowest salary.
  • There are no fixed salary thresholds and no manually assigned player values.
  • The final list is confirmed after the completed regular season.
⚖️ Fair Salary — no Value Bonus 💎 Value Bonus — +5 additional increase

During the Season

The Value Board may publish informative interim lists showing which players would currently qualify for the +5 IceCore Value Bonus based on the latest standings and salary data. These lists are not final decisions; they are a snapshot of the current salary economy.
